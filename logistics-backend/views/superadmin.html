What happens when a tenant is activated?
Once a tenant subscribes and creates their admin account, the system automatically provisions: (1) an isolated admin dashboard scoped to their tenant_id, (2) a private user registration page at their unique workspace URL, (3) all data tables row-locked to their tenant — no cross-tenant access is possible.
